Featured Project / AI Hardware

ClawChip AI Recorder

A pocket AI recorder with a quiet e-paper display. It captures what you say, processes it with AI, and keeps the next useful action visible—without forcing every thought through a keyboard.

Visit ClawChip ↗ Back to projects See SkillFit

What problem does it explore?

Many AI use cases begin with voice: meetings, field notes, spontaneous ideas, and long reflections. ClawChip asks what happens when AI hardware starts from that input path instead of a chat box.

My product focus

  • Define where hardware meaningfully improves the AI workflow.
  • Design the loop from input capture to AI result display.
  • Turn abstract AI capability into a physical, repeatable use case.

Short answer

ClawChip AI Recorder makes AI easier to invoke in real-life moments, then keeps the next useful action visible on its e-paper display.
